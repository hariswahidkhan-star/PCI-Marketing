# Certuvo feature films — claims register

Five treatments of the same fourteen features, for Certuvo to choose between.
This register covers all five: the copy is shared, so the exposure is shared.

Status key: **P** — traced to certuvo.com wording; confirm it still describes
the live product · **S** — needs substantiation Certuvo can produce on request ·
**N** — framing, not a claim of fact.

---

## 1. The rule that shapes every frame

**No real question, answer, explanation or note appears in any of the five.**
Every one is a `.sk` skeleton bar or a `.blur` block from `common/ui.css` —
unreadable by construction rather than by cropping or blurring a real
screenshot. Answer options carry a letter and a bar; the bar is never text.

That is enforced in the shared kit rather than left to each film, so no
treatment can break it by accident, and it is why the end frame of all five
carries:

> Product views are illustrative: every question, option, explanation and note
> shown is an abstract placeholder, not real exam content. All third-party names
> and marks are the property of their respective owners. Preparation does not
> guarantee a pass.

**That line must stay on any cut, crop or re-edit**, including social versions.

### 2.1 is the one to settle before publication

Three of the five say **"inside every course"**. That makes it a claim about
each course Certuvo sells, not about the platform in aggregate. If the true
figure is lower for some credentials, the honest fixes are to say "thousands
across the platform" — and change the on-screen line to match — or to drop the
number and keep "a bank of verified questions", which the visuals already carry
on their own. No exact figure is shown anywhere; the bank is drawn as a field of
chips filling rather than a counter, precisely because a counter states a number
Certuvo has not published.

### 2.10 is worth a second look

"Notes, kept beside the question that prompted them" is a specific product
behaviour. If notes are a general notebook rather than attached per question,
soften it to "course notes, in the same place as everything else".

## 3. What is on screen that is not a claim

- **The study-room people are invented.** Initials on coloured discs, no
  photographs, no testimonials, no headcount. Abstract avatars were chosen over
  stock faces deliberately: stock faces read as stock, abstraction reads as
  privacy. No claim is made that any of them exist.
- **Every timer, question counter, section count and clock is furniture.**
  "Question 14 of 20", "20:00", "Section 1 of 8", "00:29 on call", the times of
  day in treatment 3 — all illustrative, all covered by the end frame.
- **The analytics carry no numbers.** No score, no percentage, no axis values.
  Rising bars, a curve, a dashed exam-ready line, one short bar flagged. They
  illustrate that the product measures readiness by domain. They do not predict,
  promise or imply any outcome. Domain names are generic ("Domain 1") or real CMA
  Part 1 domains used as a plausible example.
- **The AI Coach exchange** in treatments 1 and 3 is written for the film to
  demonstrate the Socratic behaviour. It is not a transcript.

## 4. Red

Rationed to four jobs across all five: a timer running low, a detected
knowledge gap, a live indicator, and the numbered tag. Treatment 3 adds a
fifth — the wrong-answer marker — because it is the only one of the five that
shows a student getting something wrong.

A colour used for everything signals nothing, which is why this is written down
rather than left to taste. The reference treatment originally used red for a
mentor marker; treatment 5 flagged it as outside the rule and the reference
changed, not the exception.

## 5. Deliberately not said

- No pass rates, pass guarantees or "easy".
- No student counts, room headcounts, completion rates, defect rates or streak
  figures.
- No salary or earnings claims, no readiness score or percentage.
- No price, discount or "from £X".
- No claim of approval, accreditation, endorsement or partnership by any
  awarding body. **These five films name no credential at all** — unlike the
  homepage film, they do not show the CMA, CPA, CFA, CIA, CISA, NCLEX or PMP
  marks, so none of that trademark exposure applies here.

## 6. Music and voice

The three narrated treatments use ElevenLabs' stock **Nassim** voice
(`eleven_v3`). No real person's voice is cloned and no presenter appears. The
bed (`music/feature-bed.mp3`) is generated in Certuvo's own ElevenLabs workspace
with `eleven_music_v2` and carries no third-party credit line.
