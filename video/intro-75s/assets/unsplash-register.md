## Scene recommendations

### S1 — Ambition / infrastructure establishing
Open wide and cold, then cut down in scale.
1. `Vqs7dkLBpmk` — Castlefield viaduct, Manchester. Real operating rail in a real city; internationally legible without being American.
2. `yx20mpDyr2I` — Hong Kong container port at dusk. The navy hero frame; sits naturally against #0F172A.
3. `-IaTiYqRTL8` — Belgian North Sea wind. Contemporary capital programme, muted marine palette.
4. `w6X7XaolqA0` — dam aerial. Civil scale and water; breaks the blue with teal/green.
Hold `yETqkLnhsUI` (pylons at sunset) as the warm outlier if S1 runs too cold.

### S2 — The six disciplines (scope / schedule / cost / finance / risk / performance)
One frame per discipline, cut fast:
- **Scope** — `HtBlQdxfG9k` (drafting with scale rule)
- **Schedule** — `gyrKtgqMChY` (site progress review on tablet); back-up `qWwpHwip31M`
- **Cost** — `8wLZi9OhsWU` (calculator + laptop)
- **Finance** — `heiYgqp0Tsk` (printed financial report on desk)
- **Risk** — `NbEVXgI5C6U` (check sheet at control panel)
- **Performance** — `hpjSkU2UYSU` (statistics on screen)
This set is deliberately object-led so six cuts land in a few seconds without face-fatigue.

### S3 — AI analysis: speed vs reliability
- `hoivM01c-vg` — engineer designing on screen (the "speed" side, but human-driven)
- `TtMKq3lJm-U` — multi-monitor analyst, low light (volume of data)
- `C3DfIgig1j8` — the control room (systems that must not fail; the reliability counterweight)
Sequence speed → volume → supervision, so the section lands on reliability, not on the machine.

### S4 — Evidence, governance, human judgment
- `NbEVXgI5C6U` — the signed check sheet. This is the single strongest "evidence" frame in the set.
- `8iDaJg7TPz4` — inspector with clipboard (independent scrutiny)
- `xYCBw1uIP_M` — two engineers over a marked-up map (judgement being exercised jointly)
- `1sk5Y2MK79Q` — technical challenge between two engineers
Close the beat on a human face making a call, not on a document.

### S5 — PCI AI institutional identity
Use restrained, near-abstract, brand-compatible grounds behind the wordmark:
- `yx20mpDyr2I` (navy port at dusk) or `Ac97OqAWDvg` (filtration-pond geometry) as a slow-drift plate
- `ToZNFpgubG0` (row of national flags) for the "Global, Inc." line — institutional without a flag of its own
Keep crimson #C13329 to the logo/rule only; none of these plates fight it.

### S6 — The credential suite
Faces and study, not certificates on a wall:
- `vEoMKBdUIzs` — engineer in hard hat leading a team (the practitioner the credential is for)
- `zIL_CiC-bT0` — engineer marking up work with a colleague (competence being applied)
- `aL2rxQhEfAM` — engineer running tests on a laptop (assessed practice)
- `YsJUxCIW14U` — a team of peers (the community the credential admits you to)

### S7 — Professionals deciding: trust, challenge, accountable
- `Q80LYxv_Tbs` — a room listening to one person speak. This is your "accountable" frame.
- `ftCWdZOFZqo` — working session, mixed team (challenge in the room)
- `df7erzy97sg` — two engineers in genuine disagreement-shaped conversation (challenge, close)
- `smgTvepind4` — the distributed decision (international governance) if the closing line is global
End on `Q80LYxv_Tbs` or `df7erzy97sg` — both hold a human decision without a smile at camera.
