# Posting kit

Three platforms, three genuinely different metadata models. The same file
posted three ways with the same caption wastes two of them.

Everything below is drafted to the claims register. Do not add a statistic,
an accreditation word, or an outcome claim when you paste it.

---

## Before anything goes out

1. **Create the code** exactly as `OFFER-SPEC.md` says — `FOUNDINGFREE`, in
   capitals, `founding_route` set, `requires_application = 0`. Test it with a
   throwaway account.
2. **Confirm `info@pciai.org` receives mail** and that somebody reads it. It
   appears at the end of all ten and it appears nowhere in the platform today.
3. **Decide the max_uses cap** deliberately. Uncapped is fine; a cap that is
   hit mid-campaign turns every later view into a dead end.

## LinkedIn — the primary channel for this audience

Use the **4:5** files. Not 16:9 — landscape is the weakest of the three ratios
in a mobile feed, and LinkedIn is the only one of these platforms with a real
desktop audience, which 4:5 also survives.

**Post from a named person's profile, not only the institute page.** Personal
profiles out-distribute company pages on LinkedIn; the direction is not
seriously disputed even where the specific multiples are vendor numbers. Ideally
the same named person appears in the video — see "the one upgrade worth buying"
below.

**Put no link in the post body.** LinkedIn's own product people say link posts
are not penalised; independent measurement claims around a 60% reach reduction.
Those cannot both be right, and you do not need to resolve it: the URL is
on screen at the end of every film and belongs in your profile. You lose almost
nothing if LinkedIn is right and avoid a large haircut if the measurers are.

The first two lines are the headline — everything after is truncated behind
"…more". Make them carry the argument.

**V1 — the case**
> AI will write your schedule in about four seconds.
> It will not sign it.
>
> A model can't know the assumption nobody wrote down, or tell you the float
> isn't really there. That judgement is still a person's — and until now there
> was no standard that assessed it.
>
> That's what we built. Three credentials, a 13-domain body of knowledge, and
> every credential publicly verifiable.
>
> Founding enrolment is open. Details at the end of the video.
>
> #ProjectControls #ProjectManagement #AI

**V2 — the three credentials**
> PCL-AI, PFL-AI, PML-AI. Three credentials, and most people only need one.
>
> Which one depends on what you actually carry: the controls function, the
> forecast, or the programme. 60 seconds on how they differ.
>
> #ProjectControls #CostEngineering #ProjectManagement

**V3 — what the exam assesses**
> Most certification marketing won't tell you what's on the exam.
>
> Ours is assessed against a published 13-domain body of knowledge, and what
> it tests is judgement over AI output — not recall.
>
> #ProjectControls #Certification #AI

**V4 — verification**
> A credential nobody can check isn't a credential. It's a claim.
>
> Every PCI AI credential is publicly verifiable — anyone can confirm it,
> including the person deciding whether to believe your CV.
>
> #ProjectControls #Hiring #Credentials

**V5 — the founding offer**
> We're in our founding stage, so founding enrolment is free — membership,
> study access and exam enrolment.
>
> It's free because we're early, not as a promotion. Create an account and
> apply FOUNDINGFREE. Questions: info@pciai.org
>
> #ProjectControls #ProjectManagement #AI

Three hashtags, not thirty. **No engagement bait** — no "comment YES", no
agree/disagree polls. It is detected and penalised, and it poisons who the
algorithm then shows you to.

**Watch:** completion rate and comments in the first hour. Not impressions.

## Instagram Reels

Use the **9:16** files. Cover frame matters here and nowhere else — Instagram
crops the grid to 3:4 and other surfaces to 1:1, so the cover has to survive a
centre-crop both ways. `build/covers/` has one per reel.

**Music:** a business account is confined to the Meta Sound Collection. Do not
use trending commercial audio — the licence does not cover it and the downside
is muting, takedown or a reach limit. Pick something restrained from the
collection; the films are built to work silent, so music is a bonus, not a
load-bearing part.

**Never post a file carrying another platform's watermark.** It is openly
downranked, and borders or speed changes do not launder it.

Captions carry topic relevance, so say the thing rather than teasing it:

- **R1** — AI will write the schedule. It won't sign it. That part is still yours.
- **R2** — Three credentials. Most people need one. Here's which.
- **R3** — Thirteen domains. What the assessment actually covers.
- **R4** — Can anyone actually check the credentials on your CV?
- **R5** — Founding enrolment is free. Here's exactly what that means.

Then in each: `Founding enrolment: apply FOUNDINGFREE for free membership,
study access and exam enrolment. info@pciai.org`

**Watch:** sends per reach and 3-second retention. Sends drive non-follower
distribution; likes mostly affect your existing followers.

## YouTube Shorts

Use the **9:16** files. Here the **title is the ranking asset** — front-load
the keywords into the first 55–65 characters, because that is what search
matches and what gets truncated.

| Reel | Title |
|---|---|
| R1 | Project controls in 2026: AI proposes, the professional disposes |
| R2 | PCL-AI, PFL-AI, PML-AI: which project controls credential fits |
| R3 | What a project controls AI credential actually assesses: 13 domains |
| R4 | Can anyone verify the credentials on your CV? |
| R5 | Free founding enrolment: PCI AI membership, study and exam |

First ~100 characters of the description carry the topic. Two or three
hashtags, no more.

**Watch:** engaged views and the retention curve — not raw views, which stopped
being comparable after the 2025 counting change.

## The one upgrade worth buying

The strongest evidence in the whole research pass is LinkedIn's own study of
13,000 B2B video ads: an **executive on screen lifts engagement 53%**, a
subject-matter expert 40%, and emotionally resonant video doubles completions —
while only **7% of B2B video contains any human emotion at all**.

These ten films have no human face in them. That is the single biggest gap in
the set, and it is not a gap motion graphics can close.

The cheap fix is free: **have a named, titled person at PCI record the five
4:5 scripts to camera on a phone**, and cut the existing graphics around them.
The captions in each `scene.html` are already written as a narration script.

The paid fix is a synthetic presenter, which costs real money and needs your
approval before anything is spent. It is also strictly worse than a real person
from your own institute, for a body whose entire product is trust.
