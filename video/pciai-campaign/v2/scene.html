Three credentials.
Which one is yours?
PCL-AI, PFL-AI, PML-AI — what each one is for, and who each one is for.
01 / 03 · The flagship
PCL-AI
PCI AI Project Controls Leader
This is you if
you own the cost baseline and the schedule under it — planner, cost engineer, project controls lead.
Not this one if you sign the forecast rather than build the baseline it rests on — that is PFL-AI.
02 / 03 · The money
PFL-AI
PCI AI Project Finance Leader
This is you if
the forecast goes out with your name on it — cost controller, commercial lead, the one who explains the variance.
Not this one if the programme itself is the thing you answer for — that is PML-AI.
03 / 03 · The delivery
PML-AI
PCI Project Management Leader – AI
This is you if
the programme is yours to answer for — scope, risk, change, and the call to carry on or stop. Project managers, delivery leads.
Not this one if you build the baseline or own the forecast others report against — PCL-AI or PFL-AI.
