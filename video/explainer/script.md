# PCI AI — the explainer

A chaptered film: why PCI exists, what its objective is, and what each of the
three credentials is for. Built on the brand system, voiced by Holden, every claim
traced to a live page or the platform's own FAQ seed.

## Chapters

| # | Chapter | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Ident | PCI AI. The Project Controls Institute. |
| 1 | **Why PCI exists** | Every major project runs on numbers that someone has to be accountable for. Artificial intelligence can now produce those numbers in seconds — and it can be confidently wrong. Project controls needed a standard, a credential, and a professional home, built for the AI era. That is why the Project Controls Institute exists. |
| 2 | **The objective** | The objective is simple to state. To certify the integrated discipline of project controls, cost engineering and project finance, to a single, modern, AI-ready standard. And to become the global professional institute for project controls and AI-enabled project delivery. |
| 3 | The principle | One principle governs everything PCI does. AI proposes. The professional disposes. Accountability, judgement and integrity remain human. |
| 4 | The credentials | PCI offers three credentials, as one suite, on one standard. |
| 5 | PCL-AI | PCL-AI — the PCI AI Project Controls Leader — is the flagship. It is for project controls, cost and planning leaders who govern schedule, cost, risk, forecasting and performance, with the governed use of AI. |
| 6 | PFL-AI | PFL-AI — the PCI AI Project Finance Leader — is for professionals who structure, model, finance or advise on infrastructure and capital projects. It covers investment appraisal, financial modelling, capital structure, bankability and financial close, with AI-enabled analysis. |
| 7 | PML-AI | PML-AI — the PCI Project Management Leader – AI — is for project and delivery leaders who take projects end to end: initiation, governance, planning, execution and closure, across predictive, agile and hybrid approaches. |
| 8 | The standard | All three rest on a thirteen-domain body of knowledge, with sixty-one knowledge areas: 40% project accounting and finance, 40% project management principles, 20% AI knowledge and practice. Assessment is a scenario-based examination that tests judgement, not recall. |
| 9 | Open by design | The only entry requirement is three years' professional experience, in any field. There is no degree barrier. |
| 10 | Where we stand | PCI is a founding-stage body. It is not yet accredited. Its framework is developed with reference to ISO/IEC 17024 principles, and it is building toward formal accreditation. Enrolment is open. Examinations are forthcoming. |
| 11 | Close | Project controls has a professional home. Discover PCI AI at pciai.org. |
