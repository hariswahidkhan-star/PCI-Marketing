# PCI post-launch film — script

15.0 seconds. Institution voice. British English.

---

## The arithmetic that shapes this script

A professional, unhurried corporate read runs **140–155 words per minute**. Fifteen
seconds is therefore **32–38 words wall-to-wall**, and wall-to-wall is the wrong
target for an authority piece — the pauses are doing as much work as the words.

This script is **27 words** across four lines, leaving roughly 3 seconds of
silence distributed as three beats. That is what makes it sound deliberate rather
than rushed.

**This has a consequence worth stating plainly.** The brief asks the film to carry
five invitations — contribute to the knowledge base, give feedback, share
expertise, build community connections, and lead initiatives. Twenty-seven words
cannot say five things and still sound like an institution. So the film splits the
load: **the voiceover carries the spine, and the on-screen type carries the
specifics.** Shot 4 puts all five invitations on screen as verbs while the
voiceover says the one line that makes a viewer act. Nothing from the brief is
dropped; it is delivered in the register that suits it.

If you want the five invitations *spoken*, that needs 30 seconds. A 30-second
extension is drafted at the bottom of this file.

## Voiceover — 15s master

| # | In | Out | Line | Words | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.30 | 3.25 | Project controls now has a professional home. | 7 | Warm, settled. A statement of fact, not an announcement. Land "home". |
| 2 | 3.70 | 6.65 | AI proposes. The professional disposes. | 6 | Slower. Full stop after "proposes" — a real beat. This is the doctrine line; give it weight, not volume. |
| 3 | 6.95 | 9.75 | The Project Controls Institute is live, worldwide. | 7 | Lift slightly. "Live" is the news. Comma before "worldwide" is a breath, not a rush. |
| 4 | 10.05 | 13.00 | Bring your expertise. Help us build it. | 7 | Direct address — this is the ask. Open, inviting, unhurried. Do not sell it. |

**Total: 27 words. Tail 13.00–15.00 is score only, under the end card.**

### Casting note
Mid-range, unforced authority. The reference is a national broadcaster's
documentary narration, not a product advert. No upward inflection at line ends, no
smile-in-the-voice, no urgency. A voice that sounds like it has nothing to prove.
Either gender works; consistency with future films matters more than the choice.

### Recording note
- Record with **at least 300 ms of clean handle** before line 1 and after line 4.
- Deliver a dry, unprocessed WAV (48 kHz / 24-bit) plus a processed pass.
- Ask the artist for **two reads** of line 2 — one level, one with a longer beat
  after "proposes." That beat is the single most important timing choice in the film.

## Claims audit

Every factual claim in the film, and where it comes from. Nothing here is
invented, and nothing overstates PCI's position — the honesty posture is the
brand's foundation, so the film is held to the same standard as the website.

| Claim | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Launched 1 September 2026 | Launch deck p.17; user brief | ✅ |
| Three credentials: PCL-AI, PFL-AI, PML-AI | Launch deck p.6; `certifications.html` | ✅ |
| 13-domain body of knowledge | Launch deck pp.7, 14 | ✅ |
| 25 country chapters | Launch deck pp.10, 14 | ✅ |
| Every credential publicly verifiable | Launch deck pp.7, 15; `/verify.html` | ✅ |
| "The first certification standard to treat governed AI as core competence" | Launch deck p.8, published claim | ✅ published position |
| "AI proposes. The professional disposes." | Governing principle, `index.html` | ✅ |
| Not yet accredited / ISO/IEC 17024 by reference only | Launch deck pp.16–17; `accreditation-status.html` | ✅ **on screen in shot 5** |

**Deliberately absent**, per the Institute's own rules and the launch deck's
"we would rather understate than oversell": no accreditation claim, no member or
participant counts, no testimonials, no employer or partner logos, no salary or
job-outcome promise, no third-party recognition. The market-size statistics from
deck p.3 are also left out — they are third-party estimates that need their source
lines on screen, and there is no room to cite them honestly in 15 seconds.
