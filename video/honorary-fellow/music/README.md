# Music bed

`eleven-music-bed.mp3` — a 250-second instrumental underscore generated with
**ElevenLabs Music v2** (`eleven_music_v2`, generation `Hjk8Ja9QS3tAXbiLQpJ9`
on flow `g55ABlgoCaPumqNJ3vXm`) at PCI's request for "professional background
music". Prompted as a corporate cinematic piece: restrained piano and pads,
strings from about one minute, a subtle electronic pulse from two, a proud
crescendo near the end, resolving warm — major key, no vocals, no lead melody
in the speech band. It is used by `src/build.sh` when `MUSIC` points at it
(the default now); the original standard-library score in `src/music.py` is
still built and kept as a stem, and `MUSIC=` (empty) puts it back under the
narration.
