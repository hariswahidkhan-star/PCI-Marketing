# Share kit — Honorary Fellow (PCI)

Copy for the platforms PCI will share the film on. Every line here obeys the
same rules as the film: no examined-certification language, no guaranteed
outcomes, no accreditation or recognition claims, and every fact traceable to
`claims-register.md`. Use them as written or cut them down — do not add to them.

The strongest honest levers, in the order that converts: **no fee → no
examination → PCI's own public registry → one application → the Board's
consideration.** No urgency line: the site publishes no deadline.

**Description:**

> Honorary Fellow of the Project Controls Institute is a board-conferred recognition of distinguished professional contribution, leadership and service — conferred by the Board of an independent professional body, a Delaware Non-Stock Corporation.
>
> It involves no examination and is separate from PCI's examined certifications — PCL-AI, PFL-AI and PML-AI. Every application is considered individually, and recognition is conferred solely at the Board's discretion.
>
> WHO MAY APPLY
> Experienced leaders in project controls, cost control, finance, project management or a closely related discipline — across infrastructure, energy, technology, aviation, project finance, government, academia and every sector where leadership shapes outcomes.
>
> TO BE CONSIDERED
> • At least 8 years of relevant professional experience
> • Including at least 3 years at managerial level (leading teams, functions, programmes or budgets)
> • A demonstrable record of distinguished contribution
> Meeting the criteria does not guarantee recognition.
>
> YOUR APPLICATION
> No nomination, assessment or credential fee; optional extras, such as attending an awards ceremony, are charged separately and play no part in the decision. You need no sponsor — you put your own record forward, and the Board assesses it against published criteria, on the evidence. One application — your profile, résumé, career history and evidence — reviewed confidentially by the Board. Identity documents are requested only if you are shortlisted, via a secure link, and deleted after the decision.
>
> IF RECOGNISED
> Board-conferred recognition, entered on PCI's own public registry — checkable at source by anyone, and clearly labelled honorary. If recognised, you may also be given access to the PCI student portal and the Institute's learning resources (video lectures, course material and the simulation lab), study through Certuvo (PCI's official preparation platform) and invitations to selected PCI AI events — all subject to current programme terms, which may be varied or withdrawn.
>
> APPLY → https://pciai.org
> QUESTIONS → Members@pciai.org
>
> Honorary Fellow (PCI) is a discretionary, board-conferred recognition involving no examination. It is not an examined certification, licence or accreditation. Meeting the eligibility criteria or submitting an application does not guarantee recognition. PCI is not currently accredited by ANAB, IAS or any ISO/IEC 17024 accreditation body.

## Do not add

These read well and are all prohibited, by PCI's brief and by PCI's own site-wide disclosure:

- "Join our members" / "become a member" — the honorary route is a recognition, not a membership grade.
- "Limited places", "closing soon", "this cohort only" — no such limit is published.
- "Boost your career", "stand out to employers", "increase your salary" — outcome claims the brief forbids.
- "Accredited", "internationally recognised", "industry standard" — PCI states it is not accredited.
- "Honorary certification" — never. It is Honorary Fellow (PCI), or honorary recognition.
